## 🧠 What is Crusader Kings for Mac?

Crusader Kings is a grand strategy game where you don’t just control a nation—you control a royal family across generations. Your goal is to expand your dynasty through war, diplomacy, marriage, and political manipulation.

It is designed to:

* Manage medieval dynasties
* Control rulers across generations
* Engage in political intrigue
* Form alliances through marriage
